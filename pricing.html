FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I cancel or switch plans anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any moment directly from your account settings with no penalties or hidden fees.

How does the 14-day trial work?

You receive full unrestricted access to all Pro features for 14 days. If FITNEXA AI doesn't elevate your athletic performance, simply cancel before the trial concludes and you will not be charged.

Do I need specialized equipment to use FITNEXA?

Not at all. During your 8-step onboarding, you can select 'Bodyweight Only', 'Dumbbells', 'Home Gym', or 'Commercial Gym'. FITNEXA adapts every session to match what you have.

How does the AI Coach tailor recommendations?

Our proprietary engine synthesizes your recovery metrics, previous workout volume, sleep consistency, and target goals to adjust weights, reps, and tempo dynamically.

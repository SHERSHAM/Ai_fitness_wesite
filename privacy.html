DATA INTEGRITY & PRIVACY

PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE

At FITNEXA AI, we engineer fitness intelligence with zero compromise on privacy. Understand how your biometrics, computer vision telemetry, and performance vectors are protected.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Version 2.4.0

1. Local Processing & Edge Computer Vision

Our real-time Computer Vision Form Analysis calculates joint angles, skeletal orientation, and eccentric velocity directly on your client device using hardware-accelerated WebGL and vector computations.

No video streams, camera feeds, or raw visual footage are ever transmitted to or stored on remote cloud servers. All joint node coordinate transformations remain transient in client-side runtime memory and are discarded immediately upon session completion.

2. Biometric Data Isolation

Physiological markers—including Heart Rate Variability (HRV), estimated resting pulse, sleep efficiency ratios, and central nervous system recovery scores—are siloed specifically under your authenticated athlete profile.

  • Individual accounts (e.g. Alex Mercer, Sarah Connor, and custom registrations) maintain isolated local storage partitions.
  • Biometric telemetry is utilized solely to calibrate workout loads, recovery recommendations, and macronutrient targets.
  • We never sell, broker, or monetize athlete biometrics to advertisers or third-party insurers.

3. Client Storage & Persistence

FITNEXA AI utilizes modern HTML5 LocalStorage and SessionStorage to preserve your workouts, chat history with the AI Coach, active streaks, and custom onboarding preferences.

You retain full control over this data. You can inspect, modify, or permanently purge all stored metrics at any time through the Settings panel using the "Delete Account & Data" action.

4. AI Coach Conversations

Prompts and fitness queries dispatched to the FITNEXA AI Coach are analyzed to generate contextual athletic guidance, progressive overload recommendations, and nutritional timing suggestions.

Your conversational history is retained locally in your profile record to maintain session context and continuity across visits.

5. Athlete Rights & Contact

You possess the right to export your training history, recalibrate your onboarding parameters, or request technical support regarding your data integrity.

For questions regarding this privacy architecture, reach out via our athlete desk on our Contact Page or email privacy@fitnexa.ai.
